About the initiative

Why cyber hygiene, and why now

As more of daily life — school, banking, socialising — moves online, basic digital safety habits matter as much as literacy itself. This programme brings a structured, in-person session to institutions that request it, at no cost.

Sessions are led in plain language, cover practical habits rather than abstract theory, and end with something participants can act on immediately.

  • Delivered at your premises
    Sessions are on-site, by request, at the institution's location.
  • Open to all institution types
    Schools, colleges and community groups nationwide.
  • No registration fee
    No cost or hidden charge for participating institutions.
  • Certificate and a book, every attendee
    Proof of participation plus take-home material.
How the session runs

Four stops, in order

Each stage builds on the one before it, and every attendee completes all four in a single sitting.

01

Take the pledge

Attendees commit to core habits — strong, unique passwords, careful sharing, and recognising scams before they cause harm.

02

Sit the quiz

A short assessment on the session's topic, checking what's understood and flagging what to revisit.

03

Receive the certificate

Every participant is issued a certificate confirming completion of the workshop.

04

Take a free book home

A book on digital safety, to keep the learning going after the session ends.

Common questions

Frequently asked questions

Is there any cost to register?

No. The workshop, certificate, and books are provided free of charge to every participating institution.

Who can request a session?

Any school, college, or community group can register. Sessions are delivered on-site at the address provided.

How soon after registering will the session happen?

Sessions are scheduled around the date and time you provide during registration; the team will confirm availability after you submit the form.

Is prior knowledge of cybersecurity required?

No. The session is designed for beginners and covers practical habits in plain language.

Bring the workshop to your institution

Registration takes about two minutes. You'll need your institution's name, a contact number, and a preferred date, time, and address.
